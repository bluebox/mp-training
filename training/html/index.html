Etymology
The English word snake comes from Old English snaca, itself
from Proto-Germanic *snak-an- (cf. Germanic Schnake 'ring snake', Swedish snok 'grass snake'), from Proto-Indo-European root *(s)nēg-o- 'to crawl to creep', which also gave sneak as well as Sanskrit nāgá 'snake'.[11]
Taxonomy
All modern snakes are grouped within the suborder Serpentes in
Linnean taxonomy, part of the order Squamata, though their precise placement within squamates remains controversial.[15]
